// about the game

Two teams. One blueprint. Total chaos.

Build&Boom is a competitive party game where one side races to raise a structure from the blueprint — and the other side does everything it can to bring it crashing down.

Builders haul panels, snap them into place and lock them with mortar before the clock runs out. Saboteurs strike from above. Time your rockfall, plant traps where they'll hurt most, then swap roles and get your revenge.

Earn coins and level up after every match, then spend them on outfits. Builders and Saboteurs have separate looks — so you can dress for the job, and for the crime.
