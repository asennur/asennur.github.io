// about the game

One kitchen. Too many orders.

DishDash is a fast-paced time management game set in a busy restaurant kitchen: prep dishes, serve customers and manage resources before the clock runs out.

Every level turns up the heat, demanding quick thinking and precise execution. I built the game entirely solo: every mechanic, customer system, UI screen, piece of art and audio cue. AI tools sped up parts of production, which let a one-person team reach a polished release.

DishDash reached 700+ Steam wishlists before launch through organic discovery alone, went through an open playtest, and released on Steam for Windows and Mac in October 2026.
